# Tasks

The prototype `playground/halloween/spider-prototype.html` is the reference for shapes, colors, timings and animation code. Checks in the browser use `npm run docs:build` and `npm run docs:preview`, opened in a headless Chromium with its own profile (not the user's browser), with `?season=halloween` unless a task says otherwise.

## 1. Season, theme and switch

- [x] 1.1 Add an own `ThemeProvider` (registered in `docs/astro.config.mjs`) whose inline script sets `data-season="halloween"` for October 20 – November 2 or `?season=halloween`, `data-spooky="off"` from `localStorage["robotcode-spooky"]`, the season's favicon and the theme (stored light/dark, else dark in the season, else the system's), and keeps `StarlightThemeProvider.updatePickers`; verify in the browser that a light-preferring profile with no stored theme gets `data-theme="dark"` with `?season=halloween` and `light` without it, and that a stored `light` stays light.
- [x] 1.2 Add an own `ThemeSelect` with Starlight's markup and storage format whose Auto resolves to dark during the season and ignores system changes then; verify in the browser that the theme does not flash to light after load during the season, that picking Light, Dark and Auto works, and that outside the season Auto follows the system.
- [x] 1.3 Draw a pumpkin favicon with the Robot Framework face as `docs/public/halloween-favicon.svg`; verify it shows in the tab of a guide and of the home page with `?season=halloween`, and the usual favicon without it.
- [x] 1.4 Add the pumpkin accent to `docs/src/styles/custom.css` under `:root[data-season="halloween"]:has(.rc-hero)`, so it applies to the home page only, both themes; verify with screenshots of the home page and a guide in both themes that the home page buttons are pumpkin orange and the guide keeps its accent.
- [x] 1.5 Add `src/components/season/Halloween.astro` to `Footer.astro`: the "Spooky mode" switch, visible only during the season, storing `robotcode-spooky` and toggling `data-spooky`, and a module script that imports the decorations for the page kind (`home`, `news`) with a dynamic `import()` only when the season is active and the switch is on; verify that a guide and all pages outside the season do not request the decorations chunk (network log of the headless browser), and that the switch state survives a page change.

## 7. Sound, lightning bolts and thunder

- [x] 7.1 Add a sound switch with a speaker icon next to the Spooky mode switch on the home page, on the side towards the middle of the window, off by default, stored in `robotcode-spooky-sound` and hidden while Spooky mode is off, and a sound module that makes its sounds with the Web Audio API, creates its audio context only after a click or a key press while sound is on, and falls silent when sound or Spooky mode is turned off; verify in the browser in both themes and both corners where the switch sits, that it remembers its state, and that no audio context is created while sound is off.
- [x] 7.2 Strike a jagged lightning bolt with a branch in the sky of the hero every 45–90 seconds (the first after 20–30 seconds) with a strong flash, none with reduced motion; verify with screenshots during a bolt in both themes at 1920 and 390 pixels.
- [x] 7.3 Let thunder follow a bolt after 1–2.5 seconds and a far rumble a soft lightning, let a click on a walking, the hanging or the resting spider patter and squeak, and let the bursting egg sac pop; verify in the browser that the sounds are made only while sound is on, and render each sound offline to check that it is audible, does not clip and ends.
- [x] 7.4 Make the thunder deeper and let it die away longer (7–9.5 seconds near, 3.5–5 far) at about the same loudness; verify with offline renders that it has fewer zero crossings than before, stays audible longer and does not clip.
- [x] 7.5 Add a music switch (♪) beside the sound switch on the home page that fades in an own music box waltz in a loop, synthesized with the Web Audio API (tines out of tune with a slow wobble, a hall, each round slowing at its end, every third round running down and wound up again), not remembered across page loads, paused while the tab is hidden, ended by Spooky mode off and quieter while thunder rolls; verify in the browser the order of the switches, the level over three rounds with an analyser on the output (audible, no clipping, the run-down, the silence and the winding), that Spooky mode off ends it and that a reload does not start it.
- [x] 7.6 Let walking spiders patter softly while sound is on: two faint ticks whenever a group of feet sets down, panned to where the spider walks, higher and softer for hatchlings, only inside the window; verify with an offline render that a tick is audible but quiet, and in the browser that spiders tap with sound on and nothing happens with sound off.
